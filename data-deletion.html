Deleting your data

Delete your cloud saves and account

You can do this yourself, in the game, in a few seconds. It deletes every cloud save and then your sign-in account. It cannot be undone.

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Ashes of Midgard and sign in with the same account (Google, Facebook, X, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple or email link) you used before.
  2. Open your account: on the title screen press Account; while playing, press the account button with your name at the bottom of the menu (top left).
  3. Press Delete cloud data.
  4. The game asks you to confirm. Press Delete forever.
  5. If the game asks you to confirm it is you, press Confirm and delete the account and sign in once more. Sign-in services require this when your last sign-in was a while ago.

This removes, from Google Firebase where they are kept: all of your cloud saves, then your account record (user id, provider id, display name, avatar link, email address if one was shared, and sign-in times). Afterwards you are signed out.

What stays on your device

Deleting cloud data does not touch the save in your browser, so you can keep playing as a guest. To remove that too, either use Help (key H) > Erase character and start over in the game, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Removing the app from your sign-in provider

You can also remove Ashes of Midgard from the apps connected to your Google, Facebook, X, GitHub, Microsoft or Apple account in that account's settings. That stops future sign-ins, but it does not delete your saves by itself: use the steps above for that, or ask us below.

If you cannot sign in any more

If you lost access to the account you signed in with, contact us and we will delete your data for you. Tell us which sign-in you used (for example Facebook) and, if you can, the character name and roughly when you last played, so we can find the right account.
